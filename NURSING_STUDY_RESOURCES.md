# Nursing Study Resources

Legal free/open resources to use for NurseMate study content. Most NCBI Bookshelf pages include a "PDF version of this title" link near the bottom of the title page.

## Start Here

1. [Open RN OER Textbooks Collection](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK590025/)
   - Best hub for nursing-specific open textbooks.
   - Covers fundamentals, skills, pharmacology, advanced skills, health alterations, health promotion, mental health, management, and medical terminology.

2. [2026 NCLEX-RN Test Plan](https://www.ncsbn.org/publications/2026-nclex-rn-test-plan)
   - Use this to know what topics the exam expects.

3. [NCSBN Clinical Judgment Measurement Model](https://www.nclex.com/clinical-judgment-measurement-model.page)
   - Use this for NGN-style thinking: recognize cues, analyze cues, prioritize hypotheses, generate solutions, take action, evaluate outcomes.

## Core Nursing Books

| Area | Resource | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fundamentals | [Nursing Fundamentals, 2nd edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK610815/) | ADPIE, safety, communication, infection, oxygenation, fluids, elimination, care plans |
| Basic skills | [Nursing Skills, 2nd edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK596735/) | vital signs, BP, aseptic technique, med admin, wound care, oxygen, assessment checklists |
| Advanced skills | [Nursing Advanced Skills](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK594492/) | IV therapy, IV push meds, blood products, central lines, NG tubes, chest tubes, ECG |
| Pharmacology | [Nursing Pharmacology, 2nd edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK595000/) | pharm principles, antimicrobials, cardio/renal meds, respiratory meds, CNS, endocrine, analgesics |
| Med-surg / conditions | [Health Alterations](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK613078/) | perioperative, hematology, cancer/autoimmune, cardiac, respiratory, endocrine, renal, neuro, musculoskeletal, GI |
| Maternal-child / health promotion | [Nursing Health Promotion](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK615319/) | health teaching, nutrition, family, reproductive health, pregnancy, labor, postpartum, newborn, pediatrics |
| Psych / community | [Nursing: Mental Health and Community Concepts, 2nd edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK616982/) | therapeutic communication, crisis, anxiety, depression, bipolar, psych meds, community health |
| Leadership | [Nursing Management and Professional Concepts, 2nd edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK610445/) | delegation, leadership, quality, ethics, transition to practice |
| Medical terms | [Medical Terminology, 2nd edition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK607454/) | prefixes, suffixes, roots, body-system terms |
| Anatomy | [OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/) | anatomy foundations for all nursing subjects |
