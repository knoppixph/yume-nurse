# Open RN Maternal & Child Health Nursing - Clinical Study Guide
Source: Open RN / NCBI Bookshelf (NBK615319) - Open Educational Resource

## 1. Obstetric Assessment & Calculations
- **Naegele's Rule (Estimated Date of Delivery - EDD)**: First day of Last Menstrual Period (LMP) - 3 months + 7 days + 1 year.
- **GTPAL Classification**:
  - **G**ravida: Total number of pregnancies (including current).
  - **T**erm: Deliveries at 37+ weeks gestation.
  - **P**reterm: Deliveries between 20 and 36 6/7 weeks gestation.
  - **A**bortions: Pregnancies lost before 20 weeks (spontaneous or induced).
  - **L**iving: Number of living children.
- **Fundal Height**: At 20 weeks, fundus is at the umbilicus; from 20-36 weeks, height in cm equals gestational age in weeks (± 2 cm).

## 2. Labor & Delivery Stages
- **Stage 1 (Cervical Dilation 0 to 10 cm)**:
  - Latent phase (0-5 cm): Mild contractions.
  - Active phase (6-10 cm): Strong contractions, rapid dilation.
- **Stage 2 (Expulsion)**: 10 cm dilation to delivery of baby (pushing phase).
- **Stage 3 (Placental)**: Delivery of baby to delivery of placenta (signs: lengthening of cord, gush of blood).
- **Stage 4 (Recovery/Postpartum)**: First 1-4 hours post-delivery; monitor for postpartum hemorrhage (PPH) and fundal firmness.

## 3. Newborn Care & APGAR Scoring
- **APGAR Score (1 & 5 minutes post-birth)**:
  - **A**ppearance (Color): 0 = blue/pale, 1 = acrocyanosis (body pink, extremities blue), 2 = completely pink.
  - **P**ulse (Heart Rate): 0 = absent, 1 = < 100 bpm, 2 = > 100 bpm.
  - **G**rimace (Reflex irritability): 0 = none, 1 = grimace, 2 = vigorous cry/cough/sneeze.
  - **A**ctivity (Muscle tone): 0 = flaccid, 1 = some flexion, 2 = active movement.
  - **R**espiration (Respiratory effort): 0 = absent, 1 = slow/irregular, 2 = strong cry.
  - *Score 7-10 = Normal; 4-6 = Moderate distress; 0-3 = Severe distress requiring immediate resuscitation.*
