# Open RN Nursing Fundamentals (2nd Edition) - Clinical Study Guide
Source: Open RN / NCBI Bookshelf (NBK610815) - Open Educational Resource

## 1. The Nursing Process (ADPIE)
- **Assessment**: Systemic gathering of subjective (patient-reported) and objective (measurable: vitals, lab values, physical signs) data.
- **Diagnosis**: Clinical judgment about individual, family, or community responses to actual or potential health problems (NANDA-I).
- **Planning**: Setting SMART (Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Realistic, Timely) patient-centered goals and selecting evidence-based nursing interventions.
- **Implementation**: Executing the plan of care, including direct care (medication, positioning, wound care) and indirect care (collaboration, documentation).
- **Evaluation**: Comparing patient health status against expected outcomes to determine if goals are met, partially met, or unmet, and revising the care plan accordingly.

## 2. Vital Signs & Clinical Ranges
- **Temperature**: Normal 36.5°C – 37.5°C (97.7°F – 99.5°F).
- **Pulse**: Normal adult resting 60 – 100 beats/minute. Assess rate, rhythm, and volume (amplitude 0 to 4+).
- **Respiration**: Normal adult 12 – 20 breaths/minute. Assess depth, rhythm, and effort.
- **Blood Pressure**: Normal systolic < 120 mmHg and diastolic < 80 mmHg. Cuff bladder width should encircle 40% of arm circumference; length 80%.
- **Oxygen Saturation (SpO2)**: Normal 95% – 100% on room air.
- **Pain**: The 5th vital sign (PQRST: Provoking factors, Quality, Region/Radiation, Severity 0-10, Timing).

## 3. Infection Control & PPE
- **Standard Precautions**: Applied to all patients regardless of diagnosis. Hand hygiene before/after patient contact; gloves when touching body fluids.
- **Contact Precautions**: Gown + Gloves. (e.g., MRSA, C. difficile, VRE, scabies). Dedicated patient equipment.
- **Droplet Precautions**: Surgical Mask within 3-6 feet. (e.g., Influenza, Pertussis, Meningococcal meningitis).
- **Airborne Precautions**: N95 or PAPR respirator, negative pressure room with HEPA filtration (e.g., Tuberculosis, Measles, Varicella).
- **Donning Order**: Gown -> Mask/Respirator -> Goggles/Face Shield -> Gloves.
- **Doffing Order**: Gloves -> Goggles/Face Shield -> Gown -> Mask/Respirator (remove outside room).
