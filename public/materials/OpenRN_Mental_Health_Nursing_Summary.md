# Open RN Psychiatric & Mental Health Nursing - Clinical Study Guide
Source: Open RN / NCBI Bookshelf (NBK616982) - Open Educational Resource

## 1. Therapeutic Communication Techniques
- **Therapeutic**:
  - *Broad openings*: "What would you like to talk about today?"
  - *Reflecting*: "You feel overwhelmed when family visits?"
  - *Clarifying*: "Could you explain what you meant by that?"
  - *Silence*: Allows client time to process thoughts.
  - *Presenting reality*: "I know you hear those voices, but I do not hear anything."
- **Non-Therapeutic (Avoid)**:
  - *False reassurance*: "Everything will turn out fine."
  - *Giving advice*: "You should just break up with them."
  - *Asking 'Why'*: "Why did you do that?" (causes defensiveness).
  - *Belittling feelings*: "Everyone feels stressed sometimes."

## 2. Anxiety Levels & Nursing Interventions
- **Mild**: Alert, optimal for learning. Intervention: Problem-solving, relaxation.
- **Moderate**: Perceptual field narrowed, selective inattention. Intervention: Clarify, short sentences.
- **Severe**: Perceptual field greatly reduced, hyperventilation, rapid pulse. Intervention: Stay with client, simple direct instructions, calm environment.
- **Panic**: Inability to process, terror, dysregulated behavior. Intervention: Ensure immediate safety, stay with client, speak in concise 2-3 word sentences, minimize stimuli.

## 3. Psychopharmacology & Safety
- **Lithium**: Therapeutic range **0.6 – 1.2 mEq/L**. Toxic level > 1.5 mEq/L (ataxia, tremors, diarrhea, confusion). Maintain adequate hydration and consistent sodium intake.
- **SSRIs** (Sertraline, Fluoxetine, Escitalopram): Watch for **Serotonin Syndrome** (agitation, hyperreflexia, hyperthermia, tremors).
- **Antipsychotics** (Haloperidol, Risperidone): Watch for **Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome (NMS)** (high fever, severe muscle rigidity, altered mental status) and Extrapyramidal Symptoms (EPS).
